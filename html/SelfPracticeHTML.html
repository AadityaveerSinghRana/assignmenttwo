Article Talk:Ramayana
From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Ramayana is an ancient Indian epic poem which narrates the struggle of the divine prince Rama to rescue his wife Sita from the demon king Ravana. It was originally composed by the sage Valmiki and forms an important part of the Hindu canon. The Ramayana consists of 24,000 verses and is divided into seven books. It is one of the two major Sanskrit epics of ancient India, the other being the Mahabharata. The poem is an important cultural and religious text in Hinduism, and has been influential in the art, literature, and culture of the Indian subcontinent and Southeast Asia. The Ramayana has been retold in various languages and forms, including regional adaptations and modern interpretations. It has also been adapted into various media, including television, film, and theater. The story of the Ramayana continues to be celebrated and revered in many parts of the world, and its themes of duty, honor, and devotion continue to resonate with audiences today.
